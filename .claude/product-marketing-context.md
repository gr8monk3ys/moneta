## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Moneta is a mobile app that helps people build money confidence through 5-minute finance lessons and daily review.

**What it does:** Moneta turns personal finance learning into a guided daily habit. Users start with a placement check, move through a structured F1-F6 curriculum, reinforce concepts with reviews, and track streaks and mastery over time.

**Product category:** Financial literacy app, personal finance learning app, education app

**Product type:** B2C freemium mobile app with subscription upsell; future B2B licensing is a secondary path

**Business model:** Free core learning path plus `Moneta Pro` subscription for advanced tracks and premium learning access

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Not B2B-first at launch

**Decision-makers:** Individual learners, especially beginners and intermediate planners

**Primary use case:** Learning practical personal finance in short daily sessions without feeling overwhelmed by jargon or misinformation

**Jobs to be done:**
- Help me understand money basics without feeling intimidated.
- Help me build consistent financial habits in a format I can stick with.
- Help me get better at real-life financial decisions before the stakes get higher.

**Use cases:**
- A beginner learning budgeting, credit, and saving fundamentals
- A young worker trying to make smarter everyday money decisions
- A planner who wants a guided path into investing and retirement basics
- A user who wants structure, repetition, and progress tracking instead of passive content consumption

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Most people need better money skills, but finance education is either too boring, too overwhelming, too hype-driven, or too disconnected from daily decisions.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Free content is fragmented and inconsistent
- Social content often optimizes for attention, not accuracy
- Traditional courses require too much time and commitment
- Finance apps often jump to tools or transactions before understanding

**What it costs them:** Avoidable mistakes, delayed action, lower confidence, decision fatigue, and expensive confusion around credit, debt, saving, and investing

**Emotional tension:** Shame, anxiety, distrust, intimidation, and the fear of making irreversible money mistakes

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Gamified finance literacy apps like Zogo or Fingo — often lighter-weight, narrower, or less structured as a full long-term curriculum

**Secondary:** Finance media, YouTube, TikTok, blogs, newsletters, and online courses — rich in information but weak on retention, structure, and progressive mastery

**Indirect:** DIY spreadsheets, asking friends/family, or learning only when a problem becomes urgent — accessible, but inconsistent and reactive

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- A full guided curriculum across 6 finance levels
- Daily-review learning loop instead of passive reading
- Plain-language finance education designed around real decisions
- Clear education-only boundary instead of pseudo-advice
- In-app progress, streak, export, and deletion controls that reinforce trust

**How we do it differently:** Moneta treats finance like a learnable skill, not a one-time research task. It combines short lessons, spaced review, and mastery framing in a mobile-first habit loop.

**Why that's better:** Users do not have to assemble a curriculum themselves or rely on hype-heavy content. The app lowers cognitive load and makes steady progress feel achievable.

**Why customers choose us:** They want a finance-learning experience that feels structured, practical, and credible rather than noisy, judgmental, or transactional.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I can learn this on YouTube for free." | You can, but Moneta gives you a structured path, daily review, and progress tracking so concepts actually stick. |
| "I don't want an app telling me what to do with my money." | Moneta is education-only. It teaches concepts and decision frameworks, not personalized advice. |
| "I won't stick with another learning app." | The product is built around 5-minute sessions, visible progress, and repeatable daily review instead of long lessons. |

**Anti-persona:** Users looking for stock tips, trading signals, guaranteed outcomes, or personalized financial advice are not a fit.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Confusion, fragmented learning, mistrust of hype-driven finance content, and frustration with trying to learn only when problems arise

**Pull:** A clear path, short daily lessons, confidence-building framing, and visible progress

**Habit:** People are used to searching ad hoc, asking social media, or avoiding the topic entirely

**Anxiety:** Fear that the content will still feel too complex, too judgmental, or too generic to be worth paying for
