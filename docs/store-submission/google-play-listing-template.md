## Full description

Recommended Google Play description:

Moneta helps you build money confidence through short, practical lessons you can finish in about 5 minutes.

Start with a guided learning path, build momentum through daily review, and track your progress as you move from budgeting basics to more advanced topics like investing, retirement, and long-term planning.

Why Moneta works:

- Short lessons designed for busy schedules
- A structured path across six levels of financial skill
- Daily review to help key ideas stick
- Progress and streak tracking to keep momentum visible
- A clear, education-first experience built around real-life decisions

What you can learn:

- budgeting and banking basics
- credit and debt concepts
- emergency planning and financial stability
- investing and retirement fundamentals
- more advanced personal finance topics over time

Moneta includes a free path and Moneta Pro for learners who want more depth. Pro unlocks advanced tracks, expanded review access, and premium learning features.

Moneta is for education only. It does not provide personalized financial advice, investment recommendations, or guarantees of financial outcomes.
