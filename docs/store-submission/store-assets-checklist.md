# Store Assets Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare App Store / Play Store launch assets that match Moneta's current product position: practical finance education in a Duolingo-style daily habit loop.

## Required assets (typical)

- App icon (all required sizes)
- Screenshots (phone sizes; tablet not required for current iOS config)
- Short description
- Full description
- Privacy Policy URL
- Support URL

## Optional but recommended

- Preview video
- Feature graphic (Google Play)
- “What’s New” template for release notes
- Brand guidelines (colors, fonts, tone)

## Recommended screenshot narrative

Use the first 4-5 screenshots to tell a progression, not just show screens.

### Screenshot 1
- Headline: `Build money confidence in 5-minute lessons`
- Goal: Immediate category clarity
- Best source screen: login / onboarding hero

### Screenshot 2
- Headline: `Follow a guided path from budgeting to long-term wealth`
- Goal: Show structure and depth
- Best source screen: `Learn` with visible `F1-F6` sections

### Screenshot 3
- Headline: `Keep key concepts fresh with daily review`
- Goal: Show the habit loop, not just static content
- Best source screen: `Home` with today's reviews and next lesson

### Screenshot 4
- Headline: `Track streaks and mastery as you improve`
- Goal: Show progress and motivation
- Best source screen: `Progress`

### Screenshot 5
- Headline: `Unlock Pro for advanced tracks and deeper learning`
- Goal: Make monetization legible without feeling aggressive
- Best source screen: `Learn` paywall / premium-gated content

## Messaging guardrails

- Lead with confidence, clarity, and real-life decisions.
- Show that Moneta teaches, reviews, and tracks progress.
- Do not lead with subscriptions, billing, or legal language.
- Avoid hype, hustle, investing promises, or "get rich" framing.
- Do not call the app a financial adviser or imply personalized recommendations.

## Screenshot QA requirements

- Ensure screenshots show real content (no placeholders, no dev buttons).
- Ensure subscription gating is shown accurately and matches product names.
- Avoid displaying personal data.
- Use consistent device frame style (or none).
- Ensure no sandbox billing text appears in release screenshots.
- Ensure copy is readable on small screens and not overly dense.

## Final pre-export checks

- Confirm the build is a release-style build, not a dev build.
- Confirm legal URLs are live and final.
- Confirm screenshots reflect the current product copy and pricing.
- Confirm no internal tooling, sandbox text, or debug-only actions are visible.
